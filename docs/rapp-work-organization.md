# RAPP Work Organization/1

RAPP Work Organization/1 is the candidate host/orchestration profile for
operation-universal Brainstem Lenses, learned Hive compatibility, arbitrary
successor artifact bundles, complete forward/reverse lineage, bounded N-Lens
search, recursive improvement, and sovereign mutation offers.

Normative specification:
[`protocols/rapp-work-organization/1/SPEC.md`](../protocols/rapp-work-organization/1/SPEC.md).

It is a sibling application profile over unchanged RAPP/1. It does not modify
Brainstem or widen the closed Workspace/1 evaluator. Workspace may supply
authorized observations and catalogs; Hive may store approved inert packages;
Federation may transport sealed sovereign offers. Current authority, budgets,
interrupts, placement, successor construction, and signing remain external to
all generated or received data.
